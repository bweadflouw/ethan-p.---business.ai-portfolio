Why reliability
“The original photo is the rule. AI only changes the setting.”

On one job, 34 out of 72 images came back with mistakes.

Colours were off, details were missing, the product looked slightly different. The AI wasn't the problem. My instructions were pulling it away from the real product photo.

So I changed how I work. Every job starts from the real product photo, and every image is checked against it before anyone sees it. Each mistake I've caught since is written down, over 50 rules now, so it doesn't happen twice.
